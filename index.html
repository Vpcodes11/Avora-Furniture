04 / A LITTLE GUIDANCELIVE WELL WITH WHAT YOU LOVE

Good things
to know.

How do I choose the right chair?+

Start with the feeling you want to create. The Arc makes an expressive focal point, the Shell feels cocooning, and the low Conch offers a relaxed silhouette. Allow room to walk around the piece and space to enjoy its shape.

How should I care for the materials?+

Dust wood with a soft, dry cloth and protect it from standing water and direct heat. Vacuum upholstery gently with a soft brush. Blot spills immediately; avoid rubbing. Always follow the care guidance supplied with a real product.

Can I plan a room with multiple pieces?+

Yes. Save the pieces you like using the hearts in the collection, then add them to your design brief below. Include your room measurements and how you use the space to bring your ideas together.

Can I purchase this collection?+

AVORA is an imagined design house and this collection is a creative concept. The pieces are not currently offered for sale. You can explore the collection and download a personal design brief.
